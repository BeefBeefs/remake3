# Game rules taken from the manual

Source: `MAN/Manual.pdf` (16 scanned pages, OCR'd with tesseract; wording may carry OCR noise). Status: ✅ implemented, ⬜ not yet.

| Rule | Status |
|------|--------|
| Level-up gives **+4 points** to distribute on the character specifications window (key **C**); Life ties to Resistance, Mana to Power, Stamina to Resistance | ✅ points + spending, with the real per-class formulas and attribute caps (see "From the executable" below) |
| Experience comes from defeating enemies *and* completing the objective | 🟡 kills only; the level curve is the real one (195·(n−1)²+200 for level n: 395 for level 2; max level 50); completing the objective gives nothing yet |
| Items with a **red border** cannot be used yet (attributes too low or wrong weapon style) | ✅ class and attribute/level requirements (`R_Fuerza`, `R_Destreza` = Skill, `R_Resistencia`, `R_Magia` = Power, `R_Nivel`) block equipping; red border + tooltip. |
| Worn equipment changes abilities and fights better against some enemies (not in the manual text; the item data has `Bonificadores`) | ✅ `B_Fuerza/Destreza/Resistencia/Magia` (+ `B_Poder`) add to the effective abilities (life, stamina, mana, spell power follow them; requirements still use the points you invested), `B_Armadura` adds armour, and per-family `B_Goblins/Orcos/NoMuertos/Bestias/Trolls/Demonios/Humanos` multiply weapon and parchment damage by `1 + bonus` against that monster `Raza` (below 0 heals the monster). Monster families come from the actor INI `Raza` bit mask |
| **Dying**: you lose the objects in your inventory and one point from each ability; save after each mission | ✅ on death all backpack items and worn gear are lost and each ability drops by 1 (the executable's setter clamps at 0); the three legendary weapons (14, 48, 61) and item 129 survive (the death routine at `0x429e40` moves a worn one into a quick slot; it drops the rest of the worn gear on the ground); **R** retries with the same hero (level, experience, points, gold and the starting kit plus anything that survived). Guesses: the backpack is lost too (that routine does not touch it), gold is kept, retry restarts the mission; saving between missions: see the "Saving" row |
| Keys 1-4 use the "direct access" objects on the right of the screen | 🟡 keys 1-4 use the first four backpack slots (numbered in the inventory); there is no separate bar on screen |
| Walk (left button, footsteps audible); **Run** = Ctrl + click, twice as fast, uses stamina; **Stealth** = Shift + click, half speed, silent, lets you ambush | ✅ walking is 1 screen pixel per tick (35 px/s: 1.09 cells/s along a map axis, 1.5 along the screen's vertical), run 2, stealth 0.5; a monster moves at Velocidad² px/tick whatever it is doing (patrol, roam, chase); animation frames last `speed` ticks of 1/35 s; stamina pool and per-frame rates are the real ones per class and the state mapping is now read from the executable's speed routine (`0x42cc30`: speed 0 stand → rest, speed above 1 run, exactly 1 walk, below 1 stealth): standing +3/+4/+3, walking +2/+3/+2, stealth −3/−5/−3, running −5/−6/−6 (−6/−7/−6 in body armour) per 1/35 s ÷ 200 per point; attacking, covering and being hit change nothing; no running at 0; stealth halves the distance unaware monsters notice you (an "ambush" is the executable's blow-from-behind rule, below) |
| **Block mode** = hold right button + left, lets the Amazon and Sorceress shoot without clicking an enemy; **Block attack** (Barbarian only) = right button | ✅ right button: the Barbarian covers up (Cubrir pose held, cannot move or swing) and stops every blow from monsters in front of him (the block branch of the executable's hero receive routine spends no stamina), blows from behind still land; Amazon / Sorceress plant their feet and shoot where clicked |
| Quick keys 1–4 use the direct-access (light) items; Space shows the enemies that must be killed; Q objective window; T/I inventory, C character | 🟡 I, C, Space |
| **Buying and selling happens only at the Inn of the White Goddess**, with "certain people" there. Talking opens a Buy/Sell window with heavy and light lists; clicking adds to a basket; **if the basket costs more than your gold nothing is bought** | ✅ window + rules (merchant = innkeeper 27, stock = priced items the class can use, sell-back = half price: guesses). The Inn is campaign mission 16 (`?map=nhs/16`, Select map → Campaign): the Innkeeper and his guests are on it; `?npc=27` places him on any other map |
| Dying loses the carried inventory and one point from every ability; you cannot save during a mission | ✅ see the "Dying" row above; Save in the Esc menu is dimmed during a mission, as in the manual |
| Archers and mages (Arquero 23, Mago 25, Zelos 55: INI `Tipo_IA` 3) shoot from a distance; the Amazon's bow fires arrows | ✅ `projectiles.ts`: arrows (painted, no sprite decoded) and bolts (additive glow + trail) fly at the executable's speeds (hero arrow 10 px/tick, Sorceress bolt 8, Arquero / Mago / Zelos shot 9: 11 / 8.8 / 9.8 cells a second along a map axis, faster along the screen's vertical), for at most 60 ticks (1.7 s, ~600 px), and stop at the first creature within 16 px, tree or wall; shooters close in until the hero is in range and in the clear (8-9 cells), back away inside 3-3.5 cells and shoot in between; a raised shield stops a shot from the front like a blow. Hero arrows aim at the nearest monster within ~25° of the aim, else straight ahead, and damage lands on arrival. Shot speed, flight time and hit radius are decoded; the distances at which shooters stop, back away and fire (and the hero's aim cone and 7-cell bow reach) are guesses |
| **Saving** is possible only between missions ("save after each mission"); Load game on the main menu | ✅ `src/game/career.ts`: 8 slots in `localStorage`; a save is the *career* (class, difficulty, abilities, level, experience, points, gold, backpack, worn gear, cleared missions). After a mission the card offers Save game / Return to the map; the world map (MapGFX 1) has Save game, the Lost Forest banner (gold when cleared) and Main menu; Load game opens the slot list and continues on the map. Which spot of the map is the Lost Forest is a guess; the install holds only that one mission, so "the next mission" is the same map again with the hero as he finished |
| Objectives per territory ("phase") must be completed to continue; only way to fail is dying | ✅ map scripts run (see below): exits need the objective (no enemies of a race left, or a key item) and say "You must meet the objective before exiting" otherwise; walking onto an exit ends the level. 🟡 the mission clock ("Add time") only extends a clock — no map header field that starts one is decoded |
| **Parchments** are read instantly, cost mana (more powerful = more mana) and are best with the Sorceress; light / storm / ice / fire / incineration / earth hit or slow every enemy in sight, life and vitality turn mana into life and stamina, strength / skill / resistance / power give +1, sacred and Gods +2 to all four, Berserk +2 Strength and full life, immunity +2 Power and full mana, restoration repairs weapons, vampire and Gods also help *nearby heroes* | ✅ all 18 (`src/game/spells.ts`), numbers from the executable's use-item routine: the strength is P = 2 × the mana points you hold (above 60 only half of the excess counts); light P/4, storm P/2, ice slows for P/4 s (infernal monsters are hurt too), fire P/4 (heals the infernal ids 3, 10, 19, 20), incineration P/3, earth P/4 hit everything inside the 640×480 screen around you, ignoring armour; each scroll keeps part of your mana (a third after light / ice / fire / earth, a quarter after incineration, half after life and the attribute scrolls, none after storm and restoration; immunity refills it). Life gives 2 life per mana point and 1 stamina per point. Scrolls have no mana price; `R_Magia` still applies. Restoration repairs only the worn weapon, shield and armour. Visual effects are built from the original `GDI/3D` textures. Ally effects have nothing to act on |
| Potions: life, mana, stamina, vitality, fortuity, knowledge ("some can be hazardous"; right-click to use) | ✅ from the executable's use-item routine: the `Potencia` range is a **percentage of the maximum** (life / stamina / mana by the `Data` bit mask 1/2/4; Vitality, 7, does all three and can take life to zero, which kills); Chance (azar, 111) refills one of life, stamina or mana at random; Knowledge (conocimiento, 112) adds 1 Power up to the class cap. **Right-click** in the inventory (or keys 1-4) drinks or reads, left click wears equipment and only reminds you about consumables |
| Difficulty level chosen after the hero: Apprentice (easy), Expert (normal), Hero (hard) | ✅ dialog + the executable's two effects: monster armour halved / as is / doubled and the delay between monster attacks ×1.5 / ×1 / ×⅔. Monster life and damage do **not** change with the level (the old ×0.7–1.4 multipliers were guesses and are gone) |
| **Durability** (`Durabilidad` in the item data, capped at 255 as the engine does — 999 reads as 255): wear amounts and chances are the executable's (see "From the executable"); at 0 the item breaks and is gone ("Your X broke"). Restoration repairs every worn and carried item, and so does dying. The Ripping Sword (14), Bow of Virtue (48) and Staff of Destiny (61) never wear. Saved with the hero; the tooltip shows `duration x/y` | ✅ `src/game/items.ts`, `game.ts` |
| Controls window: detail, gamma, screen size, blood mode, music and fx volume, background sound, key assignments | ✅ all of it works and is saved (`src/ui/prefs.ts`, `controls.ts`, `bindings.ts`): detail = render sharpness, screen size = share of the window the picture fills, blood = a red puff where a blow lands, and the four boxes remap attack/move (a mouse button), run, stealth (Ctrl / Shift / Alt or a key) and block (mouse or key; default right button). Same window from the title menu and, via a Controls plate, from Esc in game |

## From the executable (`Nightstone.exe`, read with objdump)

These replaced earlier guesses. Hero values are the engine's record fields; stamina and mana are stored ×200 (one point = 200 units), and the engine runs at 35 frames per second (`GAME_FPS`). The code is `src/game/rules.ts`; tests in `tests/game.test.ts`.

| What | Value |
|------|-------|
| Experience for level n (n ≥ 2) | `195·(n−1)² + 200` (395 for level 2; the executable's table holds 195·L²+200 and the level-up test reads entry L for the current level L); maximum level 50; +4 attribute points per level; a level-up refills life, stamina and mana |
| Starting attributes (Strength / Skill / Resistance / Magic) | Barbarian 32 / 24 / 30 / 11, Amazon 22 / 28 / 22 / 14, Sorceress 19 / 23 / 21 / 30 |
| Attribute caps | Barbarian 90 / 85 / 85 / 45, Amazon 82 / 90 / 79 / 45, Sorceress 60 / 85 / 70 / 90 (Spending a point at the cap is refused; attribute parchments stop at it) |
| Maximum life | Barbarian `43 + ⌊L/3⌋ + trunc(2.3·Res)`, Amazon `35 + ⌊L/5⌋ + 2·Res`, Sorceress `25 + ⌊L/4⌋ + 2·Res` |
| Maximum stamina | Barbarian `3(L+4) + Res`, Amazon `Res + 2L + ⌊L/2⌋ + 11`, Sorceress `⌊190(Res + 2L + 14)/200⌋` |
| Maximum mana | `L + 2·Magic` (all classes) |
| Mana regeneration | `⌊Strength/8⌋ + ⌊L/3⌋ + 3` units per frame (read from the Sorceress routine, applied to all classes) |
| Stamina per frame (units of 1/200) | Barbarian rest +3, run −5 (−6 in body armour), guard −3, sneak +2; Amazon rest +4, run −6 / −7, guard −5; Sorceress rest +3, run −6, guard −3, sneak +2 |
| Hero damage | `B + W (+ S)`. B per class: Barbarian `(Str−16)/10 + (Skill−25)/17 + L/17`, Amazon `(Skill−25)/10 + (Str−25)/11 + L/17`, Sorceress `(Mag−25)/9 + (Skill−25)/13 + L/17` (C integer division, truncating). W = the weapon's `Potencia` roll plus the same roll times the family bonus (when that bonus part is negative the total is that negative part and B is dropped — it heals). A total of 0–2 becomes a random 1–3. S (special items) is not modelled |
| Damage taken by the hero | `max(1, damage − armour − 1)` |
| Staff mana per shot | items 53: 4.5, 54: 6.75, 55: 11.25, 56: 3.4, 57: 5.45, 58: 7.5, 59: 3.475, 60: 5.75, 61: 8.5, 132: 3.45, 133: 5.5, 134: 7.25; the Sorceress's staff shoots bolts and "Not enough mana" stops it |
| Damage received by a monster (`0x436fd0`) | The blow B + W is **×3** when the hero stands behind it (same facing), **×2** when nearly behind (facing differs by one step), ×1 otherwise; ranged blows are never multiplied. Then `max(1, blow − armour)` where armour is the INI `Armadura` ×½ / ×1 / ×2 by difficulty, plus 1 for actors 48, 51, 52, 58 and 2 for 61 (the masters and the orc general; hard-coded by actor id). A negative blow (family bonus) heals. Only heroes hurt monsters |
| Monster first blow | A monster that reaches the hero (chase handler `0x434260`) waits **half** its `Vel_Ataque` roll before the first blow (easy ×2, normal ×1, hard ×⅔ of that half), then each later blow waits the whole roll (×1.5 / ×1 / ×⅔); while chasing, each time a path is issued there is an `Espera` percent chance of a pause of 20–54 ticks (not modelled: the length of a path segment is unknown) | ✅ first blow modelled | |
| Monster covers | A frontal melee blow (×1) on a monster that is not already staggered or covering is cancelled when `rand(1000) × 0.1 ≤ Rnd_Bloqueo` (percent); the clang sound plays and the monster's attack delay shortens by 4 ticks |
| Monster staggers | After a blow that lands, the monster goes into its hit pose only if the blow ≥ `Aturdirse` (ranged: blow > `Aturdirse` + 1 + 0 or 1). `Aturdirse` 88 / 99 / 999 means practically never. It cries out only when staggered or killed |
| Weapon wear | Each blow that lands: 9 % chance of 1 or 2 points, divided by the item's level (`Nivel`, integer division; no level = whole); each melee swing that lands also 5 % (Barbarian) / 7 % (others) of a flat point |
| Armour wear | Hero struck for `d` after armour: Barbarian 20 % of `d/3 + rand(d/3)`; Amazon 27 % and Sorceress 30 % of `d/2 + rand(d/2 − 2) + armour`, only if armour is worn; divided by the armour's level. The Barbarian's raised shield: 80 % of blocked blows, `damage/3 + 1` points |
| Idle monsters (`0x434090`) | A monster with no patrol route roams: a random cell within `Radio_Busqueda` cells (±) of its home, walks there at its normal speed, waits 1–8 s, repeats. Also in the monster AI (not modelled): `Tm_Busqueda` ticks (default 350 = 10 s; archers and mages 2100) before a hunter drops its target, `Espera` = percent chance of a short pause (20–54 ticks) while chasing |

Item prices: the loader (`0x44cb3f`) takes half of any price above 300 off (1350 → 825); selling back at half price is still a placeholder (the sell code was not found). Still guessed: how monsters notice the hero (the executable does it in the hero's view scan, `0x41fca0` / `0x420010`, which writes the hero into the target record of each monster on a visible cell; the monster INI `Oido` is copied to `+0x2118` but only the hero's own copy is read; the remake keeps `Oido + 3` cells), the hero's attack recovery (the executable puts 20 ticks in a counter `+0xc0` after each blow, read only by code not yet traced; the remake keeps 0.35 s), the `Espera` pauses and `Tm_Busqueda` forgetting (the target record's timer counts every tick and is cleared only when the monster is hit, so it re-acquires at once; no visible effect), the exact range test and freeze/storm side effects of scrolls, what the special items' `Especial` flag does, and what `SangreFX` selects (no reader found in the receive-hit routine).

### Map scripts (`src/game/game.ts`, `runScript`)

A script sits on map cells (decor layer, see `docs/FORMATS.md`). A cell that holds a loot script on a pack-3 object is a chest (Outside chests keep their closed / open sprites; Cave and Dungeon chests open without a sprite change); every other script cell is a **trigger**: the hero sets it off by standing on it, or — when a solid object covers the cell — by coming within 1.6 cells. Teleports and exits can be used again after leaving the cell; spawns, gates and loot fire once. Commands: 1 roll loot, 2 teleport (the `Bdt Entrar` / `Bdt Salir` pairs of the dungeon maps), 3 end the level when no live enemy of race mask a0 is left, 4 start the mission clock at a0 seconds (missions 3 and 35 only; it shows top right and fails the mission at zero), 5 raise actor a0+1 (skeleton 9, 10, demons 19, 20…) facing a1 at (a2,a3) with its rise animation, 7 remove the object at (a0,a1) (gates), 8 remove the object on the trigger cell, 9 drop a fixed item, 11 end the level when the hero holds item a0, 12 end the level, 13 play sound a0; 6 and 10 do nothing visible. A failed 3 / 11 shows the objective message and stops the script. Which of "walk on" / "activate" a script wants is stored somewhere in the record that was not found (the 32 bytes at +0x58 are all zero), so every non-container script is walk-on.

### NPC speech

`ACTORS\ID-<actor>*` in the language file is the speech: the text, a blank line, then `$0`, `$1`… answer lines (`src/game/dialogue.ts`). Talking to an NPC opens the speech window (`src/ui/dialogue.ts`); the Innkeeper's first two answers open the shop, any other answer closes it. The Princess (63) has a second speech (`**`) that alternates with the first.
