# Game rules taken from the manual

Source: `MAN/Manual.pdf` (16 scanned pages, OCR'd with tesseract; wording may carry OCR noise). Status: ✅ implemented, ⬜ not yet.

| Rule | Status |
|------|--------|
| Level-up gives **+4 points** to distribute on the character specifications window (key **C**); Life ties to Resistance, Mana to Power, Stamina to Resistance | ✅ points + spending; life = 2 × Resistance, stamina = 2 × Resistance, mana = 2 × Power (ratios are guesses) |
| Experience comes from defeating enemies *and* completing the objective | 🟡 kills only; the "Next level" table is not given (placeholder curve) |
| Items with a **red border** cannot be used yet (attributes too low or wrong weapon style) | ✅ class and attribute/level requirements (`R_Fuerza`, `R_Destreza` = Skill, `R_Resistencia`, `R_Magia` = Power, `R_Nivel`) block equipping; red border + tooltip. |
| Worn equipment changes abilities and fights better against some enemies (not in the manual text; the item data has `Bonificadores`) | ✅ `B_Fuerza/Destreza/Resistencia/Magia` (+ `B_Poder`) add to the effective abilities (life, stamina, mana, spell power follow them; requirements still use the points you invested), `B_Armadura` adds armour, and per-family `B_Goblins/Orcos/NoMuertos/Bestias/Trolls/Demonios/Humanos` multiply weapon and parchment damage by `1 + bonus` against that monster `Raza` (below 0 heals the monster). Monster families come from the actor INI `Raza` bit mask |
| **Dying**: you lose the objects in your inventory and one point from each ability; save after each mission | ✅ on death all backpack items and worn gear are lost and each ability drops by 1 (never below 1); **R** retries with the same hero (level, experience, points, gold and the starting kit). Guesses: worn gear counts as carried, gold is kept, retry restarts the mission; saving between missions does not exist yet |
| Keys 1-4 use the "direct access" objects on the right of the screen | 🟡 keys 1-4 use the first four backpack slots (numbered in the inventory); there is no separate bar on screen |
| Walk (left button, footsteps audible); **Run** = Ctrl + click, twice as fast, uses stamina; **Stealth** = Shift + click, half speed, silent, lets you ambush | ✅ stamina (= 2 × Resistance) drains while moving (run 6/s, walk 0.5/s, stealth 0.2/s) and refills when standing (3/s); no running at 0; stealth halves the distance unaware monsters notice you and doubles the first blow (all rates and the sneak bonus are guesses) |
| **Block mode** = hold right button + left, lets the Amazon and Sorceress shoot without clicking an enemy; **Block attack** (Barbarian only) = right button | ✅ right button: the Barbarian covers up (Cubrir pose held, cannot move or swing) and stops every blow from monsters in front of him for 4 stamina each (guess), blows from behind or with no stamina still land; Amazon / Sorceress plant their feet and shoot where clicked |
| Quick keys 1–4 use the direct-access (light) items; Space shows the enemies that must be killed; Q objective window; T/I inventory, C character | 🟡 I, C, Space |
| **Buying and selling happens only at the Inn of the White Goddess**, with "certain people" there. Talking opens a Buy/Sell window with heavy and light lists; clicking adds to a basket; **if the basket costs more than your gold nothing is bought** | ✅ window + rules (merchant = innkeeper 27, stock = priced items the class can use, sell-back = half price: guesses). The Inn map is not part of this install; use `?npc=27` to place him next to the start |
| Dying loses the carried inventory and one point from every ability; you cannot save during a mission | ✅ see the "Dying" row above (no saving exists at all yet) |
| Objectives per territory ("phase") must be completed to continue; only way to fail is dying | 🟡 Lost Forrest goal only |
| **Parchments** are read instantly, cost mana (more powerful = more mana) and are best with the Sorceress; light / storm / ice / fire / incineration / earth hit or slow every enemy in sight, life and vitality turn mana into life and stamina, strength / skill / resistance / power give +1, sacred and Gods +2 to all four, Berserk +2 Strength and full life, immunity +2 Power and full mana, restoration repairs weapons, vampire and Gods also help *nearby heroes* | ✅ all 18 (`src/game/spells.ts`); scrolls are used up when read, cost 5 mana per scroll level, the `R_Magia` requirement applies; every damage, radius (10 cells) and duration number is a guess. Each parchment has a visual effect (rings, flames, ice spirit, glows; earth shakes the screen) built from the original `GDI/3D` textures, plus a glow for level-ups and potions. Ally effects and durability (restoration) have nothing to act on yet |
| Potions: life, mana, stamina, vitality, fortuity, knowledge ("some can be hazardous"; right-click to use) | 🟡 life / stamina / mana / vitality work (Data bit mask 1/2/4); **right-click** in the inventory (or keys 1-4) drinks or reads, left click wears equipment and only reminds you about consumables. Fortuity and knowledge are listed in the manual without effects: **guesses** — knowledge = +1 attribute point, fortuity = two random items at your feet |
| Difficulty level chosen after the hero: Apprentice (easy), Expert (normal), Hero (hard) | ✅ dialog + monster life / damage multipliers (0.7 / 1 / 1.4 and 0.7 / 1 / 1.3 are guesses) |
| Controls window: detail, gamma, screen size, blood mode, music and fx volume, background sound, key assignments | 🟡 gamma, volumes and background sound work; detail, screen size, blood and key remapping are not implemented (the keys are shown but fixed) |
